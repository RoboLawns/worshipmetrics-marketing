## The short answer: the floor is free, and the floor is higher than you think

Small-church math is unsentimental: the AV budget is a rounding error, "support contract" isn't in the vocabulary, and the whole system runs on one volunteer's goodwill. So most small churches conclude tech support is a big-church luxury and resign themselves to [the burnout lottery](/kb/articles/church-tech-burnout-cycle/).

Here's the update that changes the math: **the most valuable layers of a modern support stack now cost zero dollars.** Not trials — the actual layers. A small church can build the floor this week for nothing, and then spend — *if* it ever spends — in an order where every dollar lands on a real gap. Here's the whole plan, priced honestly.

## The $0 stack (build this regardless)

**Automation that closes tickets before they open.** [Tech Manager is free](/tech-manager/) — on every plan, forever — and it brings the [self-healing rules](/kb/articles/tech-manager-self-healing-explained/) with it: dropped streams restart, stopped recordings recover, filling disks clean their cache. The failures that generate most small-church panic simply stop reaching the congregation. Free.

**Monitoring and alerts.** The same install watches the booth around the clock — [the honest online badge, the server-side checks](/kb/articles/is-obs-stream-actually-working/) — and [pushes alerts to real phones](/kb/articles/church-stream-drop-phone-alert/), with smart help built into the alerts themselves. Free.

**Documentation that makes any helper effective.** The [Signal Map](/system-documentation/) — every device, port, and labeled cable — ships free in Tech Manager. It's the difference between any future helper (volunteer, phone line, or hired pro) starting from facts versus starting from archaeology. Free, and the single best afternoon investment a small church can make.

**One real AI session, plus the community.** The free tier includes one full help session with [AI that reads your actual system](/luminos-ai-assistant/) — save it for a real problem, not a curiosity — and the forums and manufacturer docs remain excellent for [Tuesday-time research](/kb/articles/church-av-tech-support-options/). Free.

**The deepest free layer: a second person.** Everything above makes the booth safe enough to share — which means the [recruiting](/kb/articles/get-obs-volunteers-church/) and [training](/kb/articles/train-obs-volunteers-church/) posts in this series are, functionally, support strategy. A [student from the youth room](/kb/articles/youth-group-already-knows-obs/) on a second seat is worth more than any contract. Free, plus pizza.

## When the first dollars appear: the honest spending order

**First $49/mo — Assist: unlimited answers.** The cap comes off the AI: Luminos in the app, Journey answering [910-WORSHIP](/phone-support/) around the clock, no session limits — an always-on help line that knows *your* wiring, for less than most churches spend on coffee. For a team with no in-house expert, this is the highest-leverage support dollar in church tech, and it also unlocks the full suite and [remote control layer](/kb/articles/remote-church-tech-support/) that lets a mentor help from home.

**Next: the human add-ons, matched to your actual fear.** If weekday questions and system upkeep are the worry, **Care** adds a real support pro on weekdays, a priority queue, and a monthly system review. If the fear is [Sunday at 8:45](/kb/articles/sunday-morning-church-tech-support/), **Sunday Assurance** puts a production pro on call every Sunday with your booth on their screen. Add-ons stack on any tier — you're choosing how much human stands behind Sunday, not buying a bundle of things you won't use.

**What *not* to spend on first:** gear. Small churches routinely buy hardware to solve what are actually [support-and-structure problems](/kb/articles/obs-problem-every-sunday/). Run the free stack for a month; let the [health reports](/kb/articles/reading-your-stream-health-score/) tell you whether any purchase is even needed — and if one is, you'll have [telemetry to justify it](/kb/articles/av-budget-planning-churches/) to the board.

## The budget you haven't asked

One more small-church truth: the money often exists — just not in the budget. It's sitting with the member who ran sound for twenty years, or the family that wants their giving to reach the next generation directly. The [gift model](/gift/) was built for them: a designated, tax-deductible gift through the church's normal giving covers the annual plan (two months free built in), one ask instead of a monthly line item. There's a [whole post to hand that person](/kb/articles/sponsor-church-av-club/) — and in a small church, that person usually already knows who they are.

No budget was never the real barrier. The free floor is real, the first paid layer costs less than the coffee, and the rest is a choice about how much backup your one faithful volunteer deserves. Start at zero this week.
