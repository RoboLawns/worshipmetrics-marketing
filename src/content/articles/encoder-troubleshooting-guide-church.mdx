## How to use this guide when the service starts in twenty minutes

This is a field guide, not a lecture. Scan the ten symptoms below, find the one blinking at you, and run its fast checks **in order** — each links to a full deep dive if the quick version doesn't land the fix. Keep the encoder's job description in mind: it has exactly three — **see the input, encode it, reach the platform**. Every failure below is one of those three wearing a costume. And as everywhere in the booth: change one thing at a time, and say what you touched.

This guide is part of a series — the other front doors are [OBS](/kb/articles/obs-troubleshooting-guide-church/), [vMix](/kb/articles/vmix-troubleshooting-guide-church/), [ProPresenter](/kb/articles/propresenter-troubleshooting-guide-church/), [PTZ cameras](/kb/articles/ptz-camera-troubleshooting-guide-church/), and [stream audio](/kb/articles/audio-troubleshooting-guide-church/), and the pre-service half of all of them lives on one [printable checklist](/sunday-checklist/).

## 1. The box is stuck on "connecting" and never goes live

The encoder sees video, encodes happily, and can't reach the platform — job three is the patient. Fast sequence: does the box have working internet at all (can its web UI resolve a ping or show a gateway)? Is the RTMP/RTMPS URL current — platforms retire ingest endpoints — and is a firewall or guest VLAN blocking outbound on the streaming port? Swap to a phone hotspot for sixty seconds: if it connects, your network is the suspect, not the box.

Deep dives: [Magewell encoder not streaming, diagnosed](/kb/troubleshooting/magewell-encoder-not-streaming/), and [Teradek VidiU failing to connect](/kb/troubleshooting/teradek-vidiu-x-stream-failing/).

## 2. The platform rejects the stream key

Keys rotate when someone clicks "End Stream" on the platform side, and a key pasted with a trailing space fails silently. Re-copy the key fresh from the platform, confirm you're pointed at the matching ingest URL (a YouTube key on a Facebook URL fails with maximum confusion), and try once before touching anything else.

Deep dives: [stream key invalid errors](/kb/troubleshooting/stream-key-invalid-church-fix/), and [Facebook Live connection failures](/kb/troubleshooting/facebook-live-connection-failed-church/).

## 3. "No signal" at the input — but the camera is on

Job one is the patient, and the input isn't what the box accepts: a resolution or frame rate off its supported list, a source that switches formats mid-chain — or **HDCP**, the silent one. A laptop, Apple TV, or consumer player flagging copy protection hands most encoders a legally mandated black screen. Test with a camera straight in: if that works, walk the chain back toward the real source.

Deep dives: [HDMI signal dropping or going black](/kb/troubleshooting/hdmi-signal-dropping-church-setup/), and the [Magewell-specific input troubleshooting](/kb/encoders/magewell/magewell-ultra-encode-hdmi-troubleshooting/).

## 4. The web UI is unreachable — or nobody knows the password

You can't fix what you can't reach. Fast sequence: confirm you're on the same subnet as the box (encoders love to fall back to a default address after a reset), try plain `http://` and a second browser, and find the box's IP from your router's client list rather than guessing. Lost passwords have documented recovery paths — use them before a blind factory reset erases a working configuration.

Deep dives: [Kiloview web UI access and password recovery](/kb/troubleshooting/kiloview-encoder-web-ui-password/), and the [Epiphan Webcaster recovery guide](/kb/encoders/epiphan/epiphan-webcaster-x2-troubleshooting/).

## 5. The stream starts clean, then drops minutes in

A start-then-drop pattern is bandwidth arithmetic failing in real time: the bitrate cleared the line when the network was quiet and lost when Sunday traffic arrived. Drop the bitrate 25%, kill the other upload consumers, and if the pattern repeats weekly, measure the real Sunday-morning upload — not the Tuesday-afternoon one.

Deep dives: [how much bandwidth church streaming really needs](/kb/troubleshooting/church-network-bandwidth-streaming/), and [recovering when YouTube goes offline mid-service](/kb/troubleshooting/youtube-stream-offline-mid-service/).

## 6. It streams — but it looks terrible

Macroblocking in worship lighting, smearing on camera moves: the encoder is working, the *settings* aren't. Bitrate too low for the resolution, a keyframe interval the platform dislikes, or 60fps forced through a bitrate sized for 30. The fix is three settings, not a new box.

Deep dives: [encoder bitrate settings that fit the job](/kb/encoders/magewell/magewell-ultra-encode-hdmi-bitrate-settings/), and [encoder settings that survive Sunday](/kb/articles/church-encoder-settings-that-survive-sunday/).

## 7. The video is fine but the audio is missing — or late

Encoders take embedded audio, and embedded audio has its own failure modes: the wrong input pair selected, a source not embedding at all, or an offset between the audio path and the video path. If the encoder's meters are dead, the problem is upstream of the box — which makes it the [stream audio guide's](/kb/articles/audio-troubleshooting-guide-church/) jurisdiction.

Deep dives: [audio/video sync fixes](/kb/troubleshooting/audio-delay-video-stream-fix/), and the [full audio troubleshooting front door](/kb/articles/audio-troubleshooting-guide-church/).

## 8. The multi-campus or SRT link keeps hiccuping

Point-to-point links live and die by protocol fit: RTMP tolerates little, SRT is built for hostile networks *if* its latency buffer is sized honestly for the path. Caller/listener confusion and a too-small latency setting cause most SRT grief; both are configuration, not hardware.

Deep dives: [SRT vs RTMPS, and how to set each up](/kb/articles/srt-vs-rtmp-church-streaming-setup/), and the [Kiloview caller/listener configuration guide](/kb/encoders/kiloview/kiloview-e1-srt-caller-listener/).

## 9. A firmware update changed the rules

The box rebooted into new defaults, a renamed setting, or a "fixed" behavior your workflow depended on. The discipline that survives this: never update in the week before a big Sunday, read the changelog first, and export the configuration before every update so rollback is a file, not a memory.

Deep dives: [the Teradek firmware and recovery guide](/kb/encoders/teradek/teradek-vidiu-x-troubleshooting/), and [encoder settings that survive Sunday](/kb/articles/church-encoder-settings-that-survive-sunday/).

## 10. It worked last Sunday

Nothing changed on the box — something changed *around* it: the platform ended the stream and rotated the key, a cable path got re-patched without a word, a switch port died quietly. The encoder is the most innocent-looking suspect in the booth precisely because it has no screen to complain on.

Deep dives: [which AV signals survive which paths](/kb/articles/av-signals-that-survive-network-switch/), and [an AV inventory that stays true](/kb/articles/av-inventory-maintenance/).

## When the list stops helping: three upgrades and an honest question

If this page fixed your morning, bookmark it and go do the service. Then consider what every fix above assumed — that someone knew the box's address, its cable path, and its history. Those assumptions are upgradeable.

### Upgrade one: a mapped system is a system you can hand to someone else

A [Signal Map](/system-documentation/) — every device, every labeled cable, every port and address, printable as patch sheets — turns "which input feeds the encoder?" from an interview question into a lookup, and **makes the knowledge transferable**: new volunteers train against the map, a [labeling discipline](/kb/articles/cable-labeling-system-that-survives/) survives re-racks, and the booth survives its builder leaving.

### Upgrade two: put the encoder on watch

Flag the encoder "Alert when offline" and every machine running [Tech Manager](/kb/articles/multi-observer-device-health/) probes it continuously — [Pulse](/av-monitoring/) pages your phone [the moment the stream drops](/kb/articles/church-stream-drop-phone-alert/), with [published thresholds](/kb/articles/pulse-alert-thresholds-reference/), and every service ends with a [health report](/kb/articles/reading-your-stream-health-score/) that captures how the stream actually behaved. A box that dies Thursday night stops being a Sunday surprise.

[Luminos](/luminos-ai-assistant/), the AI assistant in WorshipMetrics, doesn't answer encoder questions from generic checklists — [it reads your church](/kb/articles/ai-that-knows-your-wiring/): your inventory, your Signal Map down to the encoder's input cable, live device health, and every past incident. A dedicated encoder earned its rack space when computers couldn't be trusted with the stream. That math has changed.
