## How to use this guide when the service starts in twenty minutes

This is a field guide, not a lecture. Scan the ten symptoms below, find the one on your screen, and run its fast checks **in order** — each links to a full deep dive if the quick version doesn't land the fix. Keep the box's secret in mind: a modern switcher is **three devices in one chassis — a video router, an audio mixer, and (on Pro models) an encoder** — and every failure below belongs to exactly one of them. And as everywhere in the booth: change one thing at a time, and say what you touched.

This guide completes a series — the other front doors are [OBS](/kb/articles/obs-troubleshooting-guide-church/), [vMix](/kb/articles/vmix-troubleshooting-guide-church/), [ProPresenter](/kb/articles/propresenter-troubleshooting-guide-church/), [PTZ cameras](/kb/articles/ptz-camera-troubleshooting-guide-church/), [stream audio](/kb/articles/audio-troubleshooting-guide-church/), and [hardware encoders](/kb/articles/encoder-troubleshooting-guide-church/), and the pre-service half of all of them lives on one [printable checklist](/sunday-checklist/).

## 1. The computer can't see the switcher at all

First, split the problem: the *webcam path* (what OBS sees) and the *control path* (what ATEM Software Control uses) are different connections that fail differently. Software control dead but webcam alive points at the control link — on Ethernet units, almost always an IP mismatch. Both dead over USB points at the cable or port: swap to a known-good USB-C cable that carries data (charging cables exist to ruin Sundays) and a direct port, not a hub.

Deep dives: [ATEM Mini not detected by the computer](/kb/troubleshooting/atem-mini-not-detected-computer/), and [ATEM Software Control setup done right](/kb/switchers/blackmagic-design/blackmagic-atem-mini-pro-atem-software-control/).

## 2. The webcam output vanished from OBS or Zoom

The ATEM presents itself to the computer as a webcam, and webcams are hostage to the operating system: a Windows update re-enumerated devices, a privacy setting blocked camera access, or another app grabbed the device exclusively. Close everything that might hold the camera, re-select the device, and check OS camera permissions before touching the switcher.

Deep dive: [ATEM USB webcam output not recognized](/kb/troubleshooting/atem-mini-usb-webcam-not-recognized/).

## 3. An input is black — but the source is on

The router third of the box is the patient, and HDMI handshakes are the usual culprit: a source outputting a format the box dislikes, an EDID negotiation gone stale after a power-order change, or a marginal cable finally failing. Power-cycle the *source* first (it renegotiates the handshake), then swap the cable, then try the source on another input to isolate which side is broken.

Deep dive: [HDMI signal dropping or going black](/kb/troubleshooting/hdmi-signal-dropping-church-setup/).

## 4. No output at all

When the program output itself is dark, work the output chain exactly like an input in reverse: does the switcher's own multiview or preview show a picture (the box is alive, the output path is the patient), and does the display/capture device on the other end see *any* signal? A dead output with a healthy multiview is a cable, adapter, or format-setting problem nine times out of ten.

Deep dive: [Roland V-1HD no video output, fixed](/kb/troubleshooting/roland-v1hd-no-video-output/).

## 5. Direct streaming from the switcher keeps dropping

Pro-model ATEMs stream straight to the platform — wonderfully simple, and completely blind when it fails. Fast sequence: wired ethernet (never WiFi for the box), drop the streaming quality preset one notch, and confirm nothing else is eating the upload. If drops persist, the honest diagnosis is that a blind encoder can't tell you what a watched one can.

Deep dives: [ATEM Mini stream dropping, root-caused](/kb/switchers/blackmagic-design/blackmagic-atem-mini-stream-dropping-fix/), and [how much bandwidth the stream really needs](/kb/troubleshooting/church-network-bandwidth-streaming/).

## 6. The audio from the switcher is wrong

The mixer third of the box, and its signature surprise is **audio-follow-video**: inputs configured to unmute when cut to, producing sudden silence or sudden chaos depending on which camera is live. Check each input's audio state (on/off/AFV), confirm the master fader, and if a mixer feeds the switcher, verify that path before service — [the stream audio guide](/kb/articles/audio-troubleshooting-guide-church/) takes jurisdiction upstream of the box.

Deep dive: [routing a Behringer mixer into the ATEM properly](/kb/switchers/blackmagic-design/blackmagic-atem-mini-audio-routing-behringer/).

## 7. The keyer stopped keying — lower thirds are a black box

Fill-and-key is a *pairing*: the keyer needs both signals on the inputs it expects, with the key settings matching what the graphics source actually sends. A re-patched cable that swapped fill and key, or a ProPresenter output change, produces exactly this. Confirm the pair, then the keyer's source assignment, then the clip/gain settings.

Deep dives: [ProPresenter alpha keying with the ATEM](/kb/software/propresenter/propresenter-alpha-keyer-atem/), and the [ATEM-side keyer configuration](/kb/switchers/blackmagic-design/blackmagic-atem-mini-propresenter-keyer/).

## 8. You're switching blind — no multiview, no confidence

Not a malfunction, but the condition that makes every other failure worse: on a base ATEM Mini there is no multiview output, so the operator sees only program — every cut is a leap of faith. If your model has multiview (Pro and up), put it on a dedicated screen and configure it deliberately; if it doesn't, that missing confidence is a real line item in the upgrade conversation.

Deep dive: [setting up the ATEM multiview properly](/kb/switchers/blackmagic-design/blackmagic-atem-mini-pro-multiview/).

## 9. The macros or settings vanished

ATEMs hold their running state, but macros and configurations live safest as *saved files* — an unexpected power loss or a factory reset takes the unsaved state with it. The discipline: save the configuration XML after every good Sunday, name it with the date, and store it where the next operator can find it — the switcher version of a settings baseline.

Deep dives: [building reliable ATEM macros](/kb/switchers/blackmagic-design/blackmagic-atem-mini-macros-church/), and [Software Control's save/restore workflow](/kb/switchers/blackmagic-design/blackmagic-atem-mini-pro-atem-software-control/).

## 10. It worked last Sunday

Nothing changed on the switcher — something changed around it: a source's output format after an update, a re-patched cable nobody mentioned, a USB port that moved. The box with no screen is always the innocent-looking suspect; the paths around it are where the ghosts live.

Deep dives: [which AV signals survive which paths](/kb/articles/av-signals-that-survive-network-switch/), and [an AV inventory that stays true](/kb/articles/av-inventory-maintenance/).
