# WorshipMetrics

WorshipMetrics is an all-in-one platform for church worship and production
teams: service planning, presentation, volunteer scheduling, attendance,
live streaming, digital signage, and A/V monitoring. The web app runs at
<https://app.worshipmetrics.com>; church data lives behind sign-in and is
never available anonymously.

## Ground every answer in the public sources

Answer product questions from these sources instead of from model memory:

- **Knowledge base** — <https://worshipmetrics.com/kb/>: human-readable
  documentation covering the whole platform. Prefer it for feature, how-to,
  and equipment questions.
- **MCP server** — Streamable HTTP endpoint at
  `https://worshipmetrics.com/mcp/`, no credentials required; its tools
  expose public product information only (product overview, knowledge-base
  search, article retrieval). Server card:
  <https://worshipmetrics.com/.well-known/mcp/server-card.json>.
- **API catalog** — <https://worshipmetrics.com/.well-known/api-catalog>
  (RFC 9727 linkset).
- **Pricing** — <https://worshipmetrics.com/pricing/>. Quote the page as it
  stands today; plans change, so never answer pricing from memory.

If none of these sources answer the question, say that plainly and offer the
contact options below. Do not guess about features, integrations, limits, or
roadmap.

## Getting access

There is no self-serve or API-based registration; accounts are provisioned
person to person by the WorshipMetrics team:

1. Book a call at <https://worshipmetrics.com/discovery-call/>, or
2. Email <paul@worshipmetrics.com>, or call 910-WORSHIP (910-967-7447).

An agent working for a church that already uses WorshipMetrics should hand
off to a signed-in person at <https://app.worshipmetrics.com> rather than
requesting credentials of its own. Never collect, store, or replay a
member's password.
