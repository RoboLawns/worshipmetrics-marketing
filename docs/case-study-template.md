# Pilot Church Case Study — Interview & Data Template

_The "one feed in" story. This needs a real pilot church — do not publish without real data and written permission._

## What we need from the church

**Permission:** written OK to use the church's name, photos, and quotes. Offer name-anonymized ("a 400-member church in eastern NC") as a fallback.

**The before picture (interview, ~20 min):**
- What did the streaming stack look like before? (vendors, monthly cost, who ran it)
- What broke, and how often? Get one specific Sunday-morning story with a date.
- How did they find out when the stream dropped? (The congregation-text detail is gold.)
- How many people could run the booth confidently?

**The after picture (data + interview):**
- Weeks on WorshipMetrics, and what they consolidated (which subscriptions ended)
- Stream health: incidents caught by alerts vs discovered by viewers
- One support story: a time they called 910-WORSHIP and what happened
- Clips: how many published per month before vs after
- A quote from the pastor (ministry framing) and one from the tech volunteer (relief framing)

**Numbers worth publishing (only if real):**
- Monthly software spend before → after
- Time from "stream dropped" to "stream restored"
- Online attendance trend since switching

## Story structure (the page)

1. **The 9:07am story** — open with their worst streaming Sunday, told concretely.
2. **The stack they were carrying** — subscriptions, stream keys, one exhausted volunteer.
3. **The switch** — "pointed the encoder at WorshipMetrics" simplicity; how long onboarding really took.
4. **Sunday now** — one feed out, alerts instead of congregation texts, clips by Monday.
5. **The phone call** — their real 910-WORSHIP moment.
6. **Numbers box** — only verified figures.
7. **CTA** — "Hand us the stream" → /live-streaming.
